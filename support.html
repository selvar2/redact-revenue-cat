Common questions

Can the black bars be removed from an exported file?
No. That is the entire point of the app. Most tools draw a black shape on top of the text, leaving the original readable underneath — you can select it, copy it, or pull it out with a command-line tool. Redact destroys the underlying content and rebuilds the file, so there is nothing left to recover.

Does my document get uploaded anywhere?
No. Redact makes no network connections. Detection and redaction run on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. This works with the phone in airplane mode.

It missed something sensitive.
Automatic detection is not perfect. Hold and drag anywhere on the page to cover something manually. If a whole category is being missed consistently, please email us with the type of document — that feedback is how detection improves.

It flagged something that isn't sensitive.
Tap the box to keep that item. Only the items still marked are removed on export.

I bought Pro but the app still says I'm on the free tier.
Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID. If it still does not unlock, email us and mention which Apple ID region you purchased in.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Redact, then Cancel Subscription. We cannot cancel it for you — only Apple can. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled entirely by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to issue them directly.

I deleted a document by accident.
It is gone. Documents live only on your device and we hold no copy, which is also why nobody else can read them.

A page had a black box on it before I opened it in Redact.
Redact will warn you about that at export. Boxes drawn by other apps often sit on top of readable text, so we offer to make them permanent. If you decline, that page is exported exactly as it arrived — including whatever was recoverable underneath.

Reporting a security issue

If you have found a way to recover content from a file Redact exported, please tell us before telling anyone else: rsenthil2504@gmail.com. That is the most serious class of bug this app can have and it will be treated as such.
