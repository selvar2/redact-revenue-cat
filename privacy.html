Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

Redact collects no data. The app has no account system, no server, and makes no network connections. Your documents never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no telemetry of any kind. We do not know who you are, that you installed the app, or that you ever opened it.

What happens to your documents

Everything happens on your device. When you scan or import a document, the text recognition and personal-information detection run locally using Apple's on-device frameworks. The redacted file is written to your device's storage. At no point is any part of a document transmitted anywhere.

Documents you save inside Redact are stored in the app's private container and are encrypted at rest by iOS. Deleting a document in the app removes both the record and the underlying files from disk.

Photos and camera

Redact asks for the camera only when you choose to scan a document. For photos, it uses Apple's system photo picker, which hands the app only the specific images you select — Redact never receives access to your photo library as a whole.

Purchases

Redact Pro is sold as an in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and the purchase receipt Apple issues. It does not receive your name, your email address, or anything about your documents.

This is the only network connection the app makes, and it carries no document data.

Children

Redact is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, and erasure apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. Your documents are yours, on your device, and deleting the app removes them along with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of Redact ever collects data, this page will say so plainly before that version ships.
